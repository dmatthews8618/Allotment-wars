Plot Holders v Tidy Lawns Society

Allotment Wars

The Tidy Lawns Society wants the allotments flattened into a bowling green. Haul compost, plant a base and send the volunteers in. Flatten their Clubhouse before they flatten your shed.

On a phone: tap to select and give orders, drag to look around, pinch to zoom. Box grabs a group, All selects every troop, double-tap a unit to grab all of that kind. Turn the phone sideways for more battlefield.
Start: plant a Water Butt, then a Compost Bin (it comes with a wheelbarrow), then a Potting Shed. Left click selects and gives orders, drag to box-select, right click cancels. Fences go in with two clicks: start, then end.
